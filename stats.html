Method

How this is computed. Each row is one household submitting one TNB bill. Test entries and any billing window outside 28 to 31 days are excluded. Households already on TOU are supported from v0.6.2 onwards and will appear in future datasets.. Savings are calculated using the same RP4 tariff formulas TNB publishes.

What "peak share" means. The percentage of a household's total monthly kWh that fell inside TOU peak hours (weekdays, 2pm to 10pm). If usage were spread evenly across all hours of the week, this number would be about 24%. Below that line, a household is effectively using less power when electricity is most expensive.

A note on the sample. The current sample is small and skews toward people who suspected TOU might help them. Read the headline rate as a directional signal, not a population-level claim. The distribution of peak share is the steadier observation.
