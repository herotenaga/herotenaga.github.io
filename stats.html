Method

How this is computed. Each row is one household submitting one TNB bill. Test entries and any billing window outside 28 to 31 days are excluded. Households already on TOU are excluded pending a fix to how the extension reads peak-period data on that tariff. Savings are calculated using the same RP4 tariff formulas TNB publishes.

A note on the sample. The current sample is small and skews toward people who suspected TOU might help them. Read the headline rate as a directional signal, not a population-level claim. The "peak after 7pm" finding is the steadier observation.
