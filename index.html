Hero Tenaga
Free · Independent · Runs on your device

See if Time-of-Use saves you money on your TNB bill.

Hero Tenaga reads the half-hourly usage data already in your myTNB SmartLiving account and shows your bill under both the general and Time-of-Use tariffs. Your data never leaves your browser.

Why this exists
Your usage. Your data. Your decision.

TNB rolled out the Time-of-Use tariff in July 2025, and households were left to figure out whether to switch on their own. The data to answer this question is already yours. The tool to read it should not cost you anything. Hero Tenaga is that tool, free for anyone with a TNB account, built and maintained independently.

How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

Step 1
Open My Energy

From your myTNB dashboard, click View Energy Usage to open SmartLiving in a new tab.

Step 2
Switch to My Energy and click the icon

On SmartLiving, open the My Energy tab, then click the Hero Tenaga icon in your browser toolbar.

Step 3
Run the analysis

Confirm your billing dates and click Run analysis. Your report appears in about a minute.

Questions

Common questions.

Where does my data go?+

Your usage data stays on your device. It is read from the myTNB page in your browser, processed locally, and shown only to you. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you choose to share through the optional contribution survey at the end of the report.

What does the report show?+

A side-by-side comparison of your bill under both tariffs, your peak versus off-peak split, your usage pattern by hour of day, and a clear recommendation. If switching saves you money, you'll see how much. If it doesn't, you'll see why not.

What is Time-of-Use, exactly?+

An optional tariff TNB introduced where electricity costs more during weekday peak hours (2pm to 10pm) and less at all other times. If most of your usage falls outside peak hours, you pay less than the general tariff. If it doesn't, you pay more.

Does it work on mobile?+

Not yet. The current version runs as a desktop browser extension because that is where myTNB SmartLiving works best. A mobile-friendly approach is being explored.
