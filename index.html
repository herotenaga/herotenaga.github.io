Why this exists
Your usage. Your data. Your decision.

TNB rolled out the Time-of-Use tariff in July 2025, and households were left to figure out whether to switch on their own. The data to answer this question is already yours. The tool to read it should not cost you anything. Hero Tenaga is that tool, free for anyone with a TNB account, built and maintained independently.

How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

Step 1 / 3
Add Hero Tenaga to Chrome

Free, no account required. Works on Chrome, Edge, and other Chromium browsers on desktop.

Add to Chrome

Open source on GitHub · Privacy policy

Step 2 / 3
Login to myTNB

From your myTNB dashboard, click View Energy Usage to open SmartLiving in a new tab.

Step 3 / 3
Click Hero Tenaga icon

On SmartLiving's My Energy tab, click the Hero Tenaga icon in your browser toolbar. Set your billing dates (the end date auto-fills to a 30-day cycle and you can adjust within 28 to 31 days). Click Run analysis and your report appears in about a minute.

Ready to check your bill? Step 1 takes ten seconds.

Questions

Common questions.

Where does my data go?+

Your usage data stays on your device. It is read from the myTNB page in your browser, processed locally, and shown only to you. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you choose to share through the optional contribution survey at the end of the report.

What does the report show?+

A side-by-side comparison of your bill under both tariffs, your peak versus off-peak split, your usage pattern by hour of day, and a clear recommendation. Each bill you run is saved locally so you can compare across months, and you can delete bills you no longer want to keep at any time. If switching saves you money, you will see how much. If it does not, you will see why not.

What is Time-of-Use, exactly?+

An optional tariff TNB introduced where electricity costs more during weekday peak hours (2pm to 10pm) and less at all other times. If most of your usage falls outside peak hours, you pay less than the general tariff. If it does not, you pay more.

I am already on Time-of-Use. Can I use it?+

The current version is built for General-tariff users deciding whether switching to Time-of-Use would save them money. If you are already on Time-of-Use, the extension shows a friendly notice instead of running the analysis. Full TOU-user features (track your peak share over time, benchmark against other households, see how shifting usage out of 2 to 10pm saves on your bill) are planned for the next release. The extension will update automatically when those features ship.

Does it work on mobile?+

Not yet. The current version runs as a desktop browser extension because that is where myTNB SmartLiving works best. A mobile-friendly approach is being explored.
